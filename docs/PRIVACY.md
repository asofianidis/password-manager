# Privacy policy

This policy covers the Password Manager desktop app, Android app and browser extension.

## What the browser extension handles

- **The page URL**, sent to the desktop app on your computer so it can list the logins saved for
  that site.
- **Titles and usernames** of matching logins, and the titles and field labels of your notes,
  cards and IDs, shown in the right-click menu.
- **Passwords and field values**, sent from the desktop app only when you pick an item, and only
  written into the page you picked it on.
- **A pairing token**, kept in the browser's extension storage, which identifies this browser to
  the desktop app after you approve it.

All of this goes between the extension and the desktop app through the browser's native messaging,
on your own computer. The extension doesn't send data to any server, has no analytics or tracking,
and doesn't load remote code.

## Your vault

Your vault is an encrypted file on your devices. It isn't uploaded to us or anyone else. If you set
up sync, your devices exchange changes directly with each other, encrypted with your vault key;
there's no sync server.

## Updates

The desktop and Android apps check GitHub for new releases. GitHub sees those requests like any
other download. No account or personal information is included.

## Sharing and selling

We don't collect your data, so we don't sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.
